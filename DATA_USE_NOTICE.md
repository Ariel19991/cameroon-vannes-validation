## Excluded material

The authorization explicitly excludes raw gravity readings, client or prospect
names, commercial interpretations and operational metadata. These materials
must not be inferred to be licensed or redistributable from this repository.

The public station workbook and station-level result table have therefore been
sanitized to remove the free-air anomaly column contained in an internal working
file. They retain only the inputs and calculated outputs authorized for terrain-
correction publication.
